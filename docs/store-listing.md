## 完整描述

### 中文

Block 是一款轻松但有挑战的方块拼图游戏。把三块方块拖到 8×8 棋盘上，填满一整行或一整列即可消除；放不下时本局结束。

三种模式：
- 闯关：跟随关卡地图完成分数或收集目标，逐步解锁新关卡。
- 经典：带初始方块的经典无尽挑战。
- 自由：空白棋盘的自由练习与冲分。

游戏提供连击、消除反馈、最高分、设置和多语言界面。每天可以挑战自己的最好成绩，也可以在本地榜单查看自己的排名。

本游戏为单机游戏，不要求注册账号。游戏包含广告；在适用地区会先请求广告同意，你可以在设置中管理广告隐私。

### English

Block is a relaxed but challenging block puzzle. Drag three pieces onto an 8×8 board and complete a row or column to clear it. The run ends when no remaining piece fits.

Three modes:
- Adventure: complete score or collection objectives across a level map and unlock new levels.
- Classic: an endless challenge with a pre-filled starting board.
- Free Play: an empty board for practice and personal bests.

Enjoy combos, clear feedback, high scores, settings and multiple languages. Chase your best score every day and see your position on the local leaderboard.

This is a single-player game and does not require an account. The game contains ads. Where required, ad consent is requested first and can be managed in Settings.
